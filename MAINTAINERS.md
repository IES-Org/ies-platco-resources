# Maintainers

**Repository:** `ies-platco-resources`  
**Description:** `Lists maintainers responsible for reviewing issues, security, and documentation updates.`

## Introduction

This repository is maintained as part of the **Information Exchange Standard (IES)** initiative, with support from government stakeholders and approved suppliers. Maintainers are responsible for reviewing issues, evaluating proposed improvements, and coordinating development. We welcome feedback from the community, including suggestions for improvements, bug reports, and contributions through GitHub. If you have identified an issue, have an idea to improve the repository, or would like guidance on contributing, please refer to the contacts listed below.

---

## Responsibilities of Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing and responding to **GitHub Issues**
- Reviewing **documentation updates, corrections, and proposed improvements**
- Reviewing and coordinating **code updates and pull requests**
- Ensuring compliance with **IES licensing and security practices**

We welcome **bug reports, feature suggestions, documentation improvements, and code contributions** through GitHub. All contributions are reviewed by the maintainers to ensure they align with the objectives and standards of the Information Exchange Standard.
